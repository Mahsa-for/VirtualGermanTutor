# German Consonants (Pronunciation)

## The two "ch" sounds

- **ich-Laut** (soft, after e, i, ä, ö, ü, and consonants): like a whispered "h" in
  *huge*: *ich, nicht, Milch, Mädchen*.
- **ach-Laut** (hard, after a, o, u, au): a raspy sound from the back of the throat:
  *Bach, noch, Buch, auch*.
- **chs** is pronounced "ks": *sechs* = "zeks", *Fuchs* = "fuks".

## The German "r"

Usually a **uvular r** (in the back of the throat, like a light gargle) at the start
of a syllable: *rot, Reise, Frau*. At the **end** of a syllable it becomes a vowel-like
"uh": *Mutter* ≈ "MUT-tah", *Tür* ≈ "tüah", *aber* ≈ "AH-bah".

## s, z, w, v, j

- **s** before a vowel = "z" (voiced): *Sonne, sagen, lesen*.
- **ss / ß** = sharp "s": *Fluss, Straße, weiß*. (ß is never at the start of a word.)
- **st / sp** at the start of a word = "sht / shp": *Straße* = "SHTRAH-se", *spielen* = "SHPEE-len".
- **z** = "ts": *Zeit* = "tsait", *zwei* = "tsvai".
- **w** = English "v": *Wasser* = "VAS-ser", *wo* = "vo".
- **v** = English "f": *Vater* = "FAH-ter", *viel* = "feel".
- **j** = English "y": *ja* = "yah", *Jahr* = "yahr".

## Final-consonant devoicing

Voiced consonants become voiceless at the end of a word:
- **b → p**: *Dieb* = "deep".  **d → t**: *Hund* = "hunt".  **g → k**: *Tag* = "tahk".

## Common mistakes and corrections

- ❌ pronouncing *w* as English "w" → ✅ it is "v": *Wein* = "vine".
- ❌ *Zeit* as "zait" → ✅ "**ts**ait".
- ❌ ignoring st/sp "sh": *Student* = "**sht**udent".
